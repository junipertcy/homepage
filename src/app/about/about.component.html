I like stochastic things that are also moving. Which led me to a research agenda in designing algorithms that make use of that randomness. I came to this through chemistry: My first papers were on quantum coherence in photosynthetic light harvesting and on single molecules diffusing in membranes. After three and a half years as a software engineer in Beijing, I moved to Boulder in 2018 for a Ph.D. in Computer Science at CU Boulder on networks. Lately I have been working on exact sampling for stochastic processes, the mathematics behind thermodynamic computing. Outside work, I love exploring Colorado’s outdoors through hiking, climbing, and swimming.
